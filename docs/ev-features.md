# EV in-car display features: wish-list for a DIY Slate dashboard

Researched 2026-11-05. The target build is a touchscreen computer (Pi 5, an N100 mini-PC or an Android head unit) running CarPlay through a Carlinkit dongle, with GPS, add-on cameras, aux relays, a Home Assistant link, and possibly read-only CAN.

## What the Slate gives us (baseline)

- No infotainment screen. A phone mount comes standard, with an optional tablet mount, plus a small driver display, physical HVAC knobs and USB power. ([The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/news/less-is-more-slate-brings-back-window-cranks-hvac-knobs-and-a-screenless-dash), [Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/1843154/slate-auto-electric-truck-suv-customizable-design-specs-details/), [Edmunds](https://www.edmunds.com/slate/))
- **No built-in modem.** OTA updates and the Slate app go through your phone. ([Guessing Headlights](https://guessingheadlights.com/?p=1472131), [inkl](https://www.inkl.com/news/slate-s-affordable-ev-truck-has-no-screens-uses-your-phone-for-updates), [TechMyMoney](https://techmymoney.com/2026/09/01/slate-truck-skips-built-in-infotainment-for-your-phone/?amp=1))
- **CAN/OBD access is undocumented.** Forum posts claim Slate said it uses CAN, but I could not confirm that, and no pinout or third-party access policy has been published. Every "NEEDS TRUCK DATA" row below depends on this. ([Slate Forums thread](https://www.slateforums.com/forum/goto/post?id=221167), [AutoGuide on the open accessory platform](https://www.autoguide.com/auto/category/trucks/2027-slate-truck-is-an-open-source-pickup-44619247))
- **Consequence:** SoC, speed, gear, TPMS, motor and battery temperatures, and charging state cannot be read until CAN is confirmed. Without it, we approximate from GPS, an IMU, our own sensors and the Slate phone app (which may offer no API).

### Feasibility key
- **EASY:** software and screen only.
- **NEEDS HARDWARE:** add-on part, with a rough cost.
- **NEEDS TRUCK DATA:** needs CAN or OBD reads, which are unknown on Slate.
- **NOT FEASIBLE:** with the reason.

"Connectivity" means the phone hotspot while driving and home Wi-Fi in the garage. A dedicated LTE modem or hotspot (about $60–150 plus a plan) makes the HA link always-on.

## Top 10 to steal (ranked for a Phoenix owner who runs Home Assistant)

1. **Cabin heat alarm + pet/kid screen**: cabin temp push via HA, a "dog is fine, 74°F" screen and a vent-fan relay. *NEEDS HARDWARE* (~$20 sensor, LTE for remote alerts). Holding A/C needs truck data.
2. **HA command center on the dash**: garage/gate, house cams, "leaving home" checks, gate code on arrival (WOPR). *EASY*.
3. **Dashcam + parked Sentry**: loop recording, on-screen SAVE, motion alert to the phone. *NEEDS HARDWARE* (~$150–300 cams + SSD, LTE).
4. **Charge-aware route planning (ABRP in CarPlay) with arrival SoC.** *EASY* with typed SoC, *NEEDS TRUCK DATA* for live SoC.
5. **TPMS with tire temperature** (Phoenix asphalt plus towing). *NEEDS HARDWARE* (~$50 BLE sensors).
6. **Camp mode + off-road inclinometer**: IMU level bubble, pitch/roll, screen-off, relay scenes. *NEEDS HARDWARE* (~$15 IMU + $40 relays).
7. **Aux relay / upfitter switches + power-station control** (lights, fridge, inverter, with a 12V cutoff). *NEEDS HARDWARE* (~$40–60).
8. **Scheduled off-peak charging via HA** (SRP/APS TOU) with "starts 9 pm" on the dash. *EASY* if the EVSE is in HA; truck-side *NEEDS TRUCK DATA*.
9. **Trip computer + energy/efficiency log** (GPS trips now, Wh/mi once CAN exists). *EASY* for the trip half, *NEEDS TRUCK DATA* for the energy half.
10. **Trailer profiles + trailer cam + hitch guide lines.** *EASY* for profiles; *NEEDS HARDWARE* for cams (~$40–200).

Honourable mentions: parked-only Plex/Jellyfin theater and Moonlight games (*EASY*), and WLED light show (*NEEDS HARDWARE*, ~$50).

## Caveats
- Several sources are fan sites, forums and dealer pages, so features vary by model year, trim and software version. Tesla's 24-hour dashcam retention and the Hyundai/Kia "4 auto stops" claim rest on single sources.
- Slate's CAN access is unconfirmed. Treat every NEEDS TRUCK DATA row as phase 2.
- 12V drain is the hidden cost of every "parked" feature (Sentry, camp, pet). Size a separate aux battery (LiFePO4 + DC-DC, ~$200–400) if parked features matter.
