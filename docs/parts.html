Wait for the truck

  • Amp, $100–180: only if Slate's speakers turn out to be passive (members think the center one is Bluetooth).
  • Stitched 360° kit, $380–730: once side-camera spots are known; choose one with AHD output.
  • Data plan for the LTE modem, $10–25/month: only if you want live cameras away from home.
  • Factory camera pigtail: the connector is only known on a delivered truck.
