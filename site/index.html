The dash your Slate didn't come with.

The Slate pickup ships with no screen, no radio and no modem. Truck Assistant adds one clean touchscreen: CarPlay, charging and trip info, cameras, Home Assistant and switches for the accessories you add.

Everything on one screen

Big, glanceable controls for driving, and a lot more once you're parked.

  • ⚡

    Charging & costs

    Chargers along your way, what every session cost, on-peak warnings and how much you're saving over gas.

  • 🗺️

    Road trips

    A trip planner with charging stops, live stall checks, traffic, weather and fires ahead on the route.

  • ✈️

    Airport pickup

    Follows the flight live and tells you when to leave so you're out front when they are.

  • 🏕️

    Parked mode

    Camp lighting, games, karaoke, a tow and theft watch, and more for when the truck isn't moving.

  • 🏠

    Home Assistant

    Open the garage, see the house, and let your home see the truck's charge, location and alerts.

  • 👥

    Drivers & trips

    Each driver gets their own theme and layout; every trip, stop and charge is logged with who was driving.

How it works

Three parts, all off-the-shelf hardware you can buy today.

  1. 1

    The brain

    A Raspberry Pi under the dash runs the software. It keeps your trips and settings, makes the truck's own Wi-Fi, and serves the screens.

  2. 2

    The cockpit

    An Android tablet in the dash mount shows the screens full time. CarPlay comes through a small adapter plugged into it.

  3. 3

    The truck box

    A small ESP32 relay board switches the lights and accessories you add, and reads ignition, reverse, cabin temperature and the 12 V battery.
